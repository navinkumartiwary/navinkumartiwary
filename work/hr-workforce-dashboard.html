How it was built

  • Data prepared with pandas: joining year, status (Active / Left), tenure bands and gender labels.
  • Twelve Plotly Express charts in a Power BI colour theme, laid out in a 3-column grid with six KPI cards.
  • Five slicers (site, department, category, nationality, status) rebuild every chart and KPI, as ipywidgets dropdowns did in the notebook.
  • Sample data: 1,200 made-up employees across three project sites and head office. Download the CSV
